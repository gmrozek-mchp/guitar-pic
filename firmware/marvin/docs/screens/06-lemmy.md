## What it does

lemmy is an animated puppet that brings the performance to life — nodding its head and moving its
jaw right along with the beat that beatbox hears. It doesn't sense or decide anything itself; it
just takes the rhythm it's given and turns it into motion.

## Why the PIC32CM6408

Driving a couple of hobby servos in perfect time doesn't need a big processor — it needs a small,
efficient microcontroller that can hold precise timing while staying simple and low-power. It's a
good showcase of how far a small chip can go when the job is well-matched to it.

## Built with

- **MPLAB X IDE** + **MCC** for peripheral setup
- Microchip **LAN8651** for its network connection
